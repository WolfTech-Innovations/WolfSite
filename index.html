Questions & Answers

Common questions. Clear answers.

What is KibaOS?
KibaOS is a modern, distraction-free desktop operating system. It brings together the speed and security of an open platform with the intuitive polish of a consumer desktop, making your daily computing quiet, fast, and effortless.
How does Windows app compatibility work?
KibaOS includes a dedicated, hardware-accelerated Windows workspace built right in. Press Super + K at any time to switch instantly between your native desktop and your essential Windows programs — with zero restarting or dual-booting.
Can I try it without touching my files?
Yes, completely. When you boot KibaOS from a USB drive, it runs entirely in memory without writing to your disk. You can explore the interface, test your Wi-Fi, and try apps with complete peace of mind.
Will it run on my hardware?
If your computer has at least 4 GB of RAM and a 64-bit Intel or AMD processor, KibaOS will run fluidly, stay cool, and conserve battery.
Is KibaOS free and private?
Yes. KibaOS is 100% free and open source under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, no paywalls, and absolutely no telemetry or advertising.
